Getting started

From zero to a working team — in four short processes.

📝Create your account

  1. Open the BizMaster page and press “Create your account”.
  2. Fill in your name, email and a password of at least 6 characters.
  3. Choose the businesses to open: retail, café and hotel — one of each type.
  4. Review the summary, tick the agreement box and press “Create my account”.
  5. Confirm the email link if asked — your businesses open instantly.

🔓First sign-in on a new device

  1. Go to Staff Sign In and open “Owner activates it once”.
  2. The owner signs in with email and password — once per device.
  3. “My businesses” opens, where the owner manages every shop.
  4. From now on, staff only type name + password on this device.

👥Register your staff

  1. Open a business in Admin (Admin → Staff).
  2. Press “Add staff” and type the person’s name and position.
  3. Give them a password or PIN — the position decides where they land.
  4. Save. The staff member signs in at the same door, on any device.

Positions: Cashier · Waiter · Chef / Barista · Reception · Cleaner · Guard · Manager.

🚪The daily routine

  1. Each morning, every staff member opens the same BizMaster page.
  2. They type their name and password, and press “Sign in”.
  3. Each one lands in their own workplace — no app hunting.

Retail

Counter sales, stock and reports — the daily workflow of a shop.

🧾Take a payment (POS checkout)

  1. Sign in and open POS.
  2. Find the product by searching, category or barcode scan.
  3. Tap the product — it jumps into the cart with its price.
  4. Adjust the quantity or apply a discount if needed.
  5. Press Charge, choose cash / card / mobile, and enter the received amount.
  6. The change is calculated — press Complete & Print.

⏸Hold and recall a cart

  1. Press Hold to park the current cart and give it a name.
  2. Serve the next customer with a fresh cart.
  3. Open Held Orders and tap the parked cart to bring it back.

📦Products & inventory

  1. In Admin → Products, press “Add product”.
  2. Fill in name, category, price, barcode and opening stock.
  3. Use “Stock in / out” for deliveries, waste and corrections.
  4. Watch the low-stock alert and reorder before you run out.

📱QR Scan & Go (self-service)

  1. Print the shop QR code and place it at the entrance.
  2. Customers scan it, pick their items and pay on their phone.
  3. The exit check shows the staff what was paid — release the customer.

📊Daily reports

  1. Open Reports in Admin and pick the day (or a range).
  2. See totals, best sellers, payments and staff performance.
  3. Press Export to save or share the numbers.

Café & restaurant

From menu to table to kitchen to bill — the full service flow.

📖Build the menu & recipes

  1. In Admin → Menu, create categories (coffee, breakfast…).
  2. Add items with price, image and modifiers (size, extras).
  3. Open Recipes and set the ingredients and portions of each item.
  4. Every sale then reduces the stock of exactly what was used.

📝Take a table order (waiter)

  1. Sign in as waiter and open Tables.
  2. Tap the table the guests are sitting at.
  3. Add items and modifiers to the order.
  4. Press “Send to kitchen” — the ticket appears instantly.
  5. When the guests leave, request the bill for the table.

🔁From order to kitchen to table

  1. The waiter sends the order — the kitchen ticket appears in real time.
  2. The kitchen marks items ready; the waiter sees the update.
  3. The waiter serves and marks the items as served.

💳Bill & payment

  1. The cashier opens Bills and picks the table.
  2. The bill lists every round that was sent to the kitchen.
  3. Apply a discount or split if needed, then take the payment.
  4. Print the receipt — the table frees up for the next guests.

Guests can also order and pay themselves by scanning the table QR (Customer Menu).

Kitchen Display

Live tickets by station — built for the chef’s screen.

🖥Work the tickets

  1. Sign in as chef or barista — the display opens by itself.
  2. New orders arrive as tickets, oldest first, with a timer.
  3. Choose your station filter to see only your items.
  4. Tap an item to start it; the waiter sees the progress.

✅Ready and served

  1. Tap “Ready” when an item leaves your station.
  2. The waiter marks it “Served” when it reaches the table.

Waiter chat: tap the ticket to send a quick message (“5 minutes”, “out of stock”).

Licences — plans and renewal

  1. Open the Licences tab (or the banner) to see every plan and price.
  2. Pick the plan that covers your business type — monthly or yearly.
  3. Pay by Telebirr or CBE and send the reference — or paste the key you received.
  4. The licence activates instantly — every menu opens again.
  5. Before a licence ends you get a 5-day warning, so you never lose a day.

Your data is never deleted at expiry — it waits safely for 90 days.
