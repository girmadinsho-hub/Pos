📝 Good to know

  • Keep names short — they appear on kitchen tickets and Call-Waiter alerts, so staff can read them at a glance.
  • Match your POS tables — if your waiters also pick tables in the POS (T1, T2…), use the same names here so both systems speak one language.
  • Any language works — Amharic, English, or names with emojis (🌿 Garden 1) are all fine.
  • Test before printing — tap “▶ test” under any sticker. It opens the menu exactly as your customer would see it at that table.
  • Printing — from a desktop browser: A4 paper, 3 stickers per row. The dashed border is your cutting guide.
